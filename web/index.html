Deterministic runtime boundary

Every agent call ends in one of four governed outcomes.

Your AI agent can use an LLM, a LangGraph workflow, a custom HTTP service, local models, or deterministic rules. Mendari’s policy engine turns every call into a strict clinical boundary — and policy can only lower automation, never raise it.

  • AUTO

    Automated

    Acted on without a person, strictly within the agent’s risk cap and confidence policy.

  • HUMAN_REVIEW

    Human review

    A clinician decides, with the agent’s recommendation and guardrail trace.

  • ABSTAIN

    Abstained

    Not confident enough — or missing required EHR data — to make a call.

  • BLOCK

    Blocked

    The input schema, safety screen, or a clinical guardrail stops it cold.

Every decision writes to an append-only, hash-chained audit trail. Escalated cases wait in the clinician review queue, and every decision exports as a FHIR Task.

Bring your own agent

Wrap any healthcare AI agent in minutes.

Mendari is the governance, evaluation, and distribution layer — your AI agents are the product. Wrap any Claude, OpenAI, LangGraph, custom HTTP agent, or ruleset as a signed, governed agent with typed schemas, risk caps, and reproducible evals.

  • Model-agnostic slots: benchmark frontier LLMs, local models, HTTP agents, and rules head-to-head on identical cases
  • Standard healthcare plumbing: instant REST, FHIR R4 $decide, CDS Hooks 2.0, and clinician review queue
  • Signed Hub publishing: ship Ed25519-verified archives with auto-rendered Decision Cards and zero-PHI site evals
