What Mendari is

Every decision ends in one of four outcomes. Act on the outcome; the recommendation says what to do.

  1. Your EHR, app or script sends an input: FHIR R4, a CDS Hooks request, or JSON over REST.
  2. The pack's input schema checks it. FHIR input first passes a structural R4 check, then R4 and US Core rules.
  3. Provider slots answer the pack's questions: rules, Jev, classical machine learning or a language model, each a variant the deployment chooses.
  4. The decision policy turns the answers into an outcome with thresholds, rules and the pack's risk-level caps. Policy only ever lowers automation.
  5. The outcome is AUTO, HUMAN_REVIEW, ABSTAIN or BLOCK.
  6. Every decision is recorded in a hash-chained audit trail. One that needs a person waits in the review queue, and each decision can be read as a FHIR Task.

FHIR in, FHIR out

The FHIR base is /fhir. GET /fhir/metadata is its CapabilityStatement, and $decide's OperationDefinition says what the operation takes. POST /fhir/$decide takes Parameters (pack, and data as a Bundle) and returns the decision as a FHIR Bundle, a GuidanceResponse first: act on its outcome, not its status. Decisions are read and searched as Task, and their audit trail as AuditEvent and Provenance, your organization's only. Mendari is a decision engine, not a FHIR data server: it stores and serves no clinical resources.

CDS Hooks

GET /cds-services lists the services, one per pack that declares a hook (the discover permission: every role, and a key or token scoped to decide; never an EHR launch), and POST /cds-services/{id} decides on a hook's context and prefetch (decide), answering with cards. A part of the record it cannot read gives a recorded ABSTAIN and an info card that says what. A card's uuid is its decision's id, and POST /cds-services/{id}/feedback records what the clinician did with it, accepted or overridden, as codes and ids only. An EHR calls them with a key from the cds-hooks preset or, once the deployment registers it (MENDARI_CDS_CLIENTS), with its own signed token. With sign-in off, they answer on this machine only.

Guarding a model's replies

A pack with a guard section, such as the AI Guard reference pack, checks a model's draft before a patient reads it. POST /api/packs/{id}/guard answers {status, reply, outcome, recommendation, reasons, fallback, decision_id}: the draft is released only when the decision is automated with a release recommendation; otherwise it is held_for_review or blocked. Never send a blocked draft: act on fallback. The same check runs in Python, without a server.
