Deterministic runtime boundary

Every agent call ends in one of four governed outcomes.

Your AI agent can use rules, classical models, Jev, a hosted LLM, or a multi-step external workflow. Mendari’s policy engine turns the agent’s output into a deterministic clinical boundary — and policy can only lower automation, never raise it.

  • AUTO

    Automated

    Acted on without a person, strictly within the agent’s risk cap and confidence policy.

  • HUMAN_REVIEW

    Human review

    A clinician decides, with the agent’s recommendation and guardrail trace.

  • ABSTAIN

    Abstained

    Not confident enough — or missing required EHR data — to make a call.

  • BLOCK

    Blocked

    The input schema, safety screen, or a clinical guardrail stops it cold.

Every agent decision is written to an append-only, hash-chained audit trail. Cases needing a clinician wait in the review queue, and every decision can be read as a FHIR Task.

Bring your own agent

Wrap any healthcare AI agent in minutes.

Mendari is the governance, evaluation, and distribution layer — your AI agents are the product. Package any Claude, OpenAI, LangGraph, custom HTTP agent, or deterministic ruleset as a portable Decision Pack with typed schemas, risk caps, and signed evals.

  • Model-agnostic slots: benchmark frontier LLMs, local models, and rules head-to-head on identical cases
  • Standard healthcare plumbing: instant REST, FHIR R4 $decide, CDS Hooks 2.0, and clinician review queue
  • Signed Hub publishing: ship Ed25519-verified archives with auto-rendered Decision Cards and multi-site eval support

Wrap, eval & publish your agentCLI

$ mendari new triage-agent --id my-org/triage-agent
$ mendari test triage-agent
$ mendari eval triage-agent --dataset examples
$ mendari push triage-agent --key keys/publisher.key
