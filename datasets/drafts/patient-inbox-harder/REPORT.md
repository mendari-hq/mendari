## How each family was made

by chance, it takes a hedge before or after it (on a nurse's or physician's family:
50% of urgent cases, 30% of others), an
administrative aside before or after it (30%, only where the family's team
outranks the aside's), the SMS channel and texting style (30%) or on the
portal a greeting, a sign-off and a subject, and typos (30%).
